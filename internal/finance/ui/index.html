PREFERRED · EXPERIMENTAL

Enable Banking

Use your own registered application and individually linked accounts. The UK bank directory is fetched from your provider, so availability depends on your application and institution.

  1. Register and activate your provider application.
  2. Set its exact HTTPS callback shown below.
  3. Open your budget → More → Bank Sync → Set up Enable Banking.
  4. Link an account, choose a UK bank, and approve its consent yourself.

Provider setup guide ↗

Bank setup and sync are owner-only. No live bank connection has been verified by installing this app. Automatic background bank sync is currently unavailable with the enforced OneAccount and encryption configuration; use native Sync while the budget is open.

A RECOVERY PLAN

Encrypted backups, with your own passphrase

First open your budget → Settings → Export data. Choose that native ZIP below. Finance encrypts it before download or a copy to My Drive. Your passphrase is never saved.

Verify or restore an encrypted backup

Choose your .mfb file and enter its recovery passphrase. After verification, download the original budget ZIP and use Actual's Import budget to restore to a new budget. This does not overwrite an existing budget.

Provider private keys and the server credential store are not included. Reconnect banks after recovery. Up to 64 MiB per budget export. Keep a verified backup and your passphrase separately. Scheduled backups are not enabled.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Make sharing a deliberate choice

Actual stores your ledger in your browser and syncs it to your Windows server. Enable budget end-to-end encryption in Actual's settings if you want it; remember its separate passphrase.

Budget encryption does not encrypt native provider credentials. Those remain server-readable inside your protected Windows app folder. Use Windows disk encryption and a trusted household server account.

Household members can work on budgets you explicitly share. Everyone with access sees the whole shared budget; keep private spending in a separate budget or instance. A read-only viewer role is not available.

No external AI categorisation or telemetry is added. The app cannot protect data from a compromised Windows account or server.
